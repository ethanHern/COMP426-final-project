DealSteal is a community-powered app for discovering and sharing the best grocery store deals. Users can post new deals, browse and sort offers by stores, dates, and bookmark favorites. 

## Features
- **Post your own Deals :** Share grocery deal finds by creating and uploading deals for other users to discover
- **Endorse Certain Deals :** Like deals posted by others to show appreciation
- **Bookmark Certain Deals :** Bookmark deals posted by others to keep track of your favorite savings
- **Specific Store Deals :** Easily search for deals associated with specific grocery stores and sort based on several filters
- **Expiration Alerts :** Get notifications when your bookmarked deals are about to expire
